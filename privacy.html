Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

Obsidian GSync is a personal project for synchronizing Obsidian notes between devices using Google Drive. It uses the Google Drive API through a user-owned Google Cloud OAuth application.

2. Information Accessed

The application accesses files and folders in the Google Drive folder configured by the user. This may include file names, contents, modification times, sizes, and checksums.

The application requests the Google Drive scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive This permission is broader than the configured sync folder. The plugin's synchronization operations are limited to the folder selected by the user.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to synchronize files between the user's local Obsidian vault and Google Drive. Files are listed, downloaded, uploaded, or moved to the trash as required by synchronization.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Files remain on the user's devices and in the user's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally in the Obsidian plugin configuration on each device.

This project does not operate a server for collecting or storing users' notes or OAuth tokens. Data is transferred directly between the user's devices and Google's services.

No advertising, analytics, or AI model training is performed by this project.

5. OAuth Authentication

Google OAuth is used to authorize access to Google Drive. The sign-in redirect page transfers the one-time authorization code back to Obsidian and does not store that code on a server.

6. Third-Party Services

Google Drive and Google OAuth are subject to Google's privacy policy and terms of service.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account: Google Account Permissions .

8. Contact

For questions about this project, please contact the repository owner through the project's GitHub repository.
